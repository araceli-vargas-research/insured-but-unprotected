# Legal Source Hierarchy

Sources should be viewed in this order:

1. Enrolled or enacted bill text on an official legislative website.
2. Codified statute or administrative regulation on an official government website.
3. Official insurance-department bulletin, order, implementation guidance, or filing instruction.
4. Official legislative history or fiscal note for timing and interpretation.
5. NCSL, NAIC, CMS, DOL, or another governmental synthesis for discovery and cross-checking.
6. Peer-reviewed legal or empirical scholarship.
7. Reputable legal commentary or industry summaries for discovery only.

Every verified event needs an official primary source. Secondary summaries cannot independently establish a treatment date, affected market, or statutory scope. Archive or download source documents when permitted and record the retrieval date. If official sources conflict, flag the row `Needs Legal Review` and explain the conflict.
