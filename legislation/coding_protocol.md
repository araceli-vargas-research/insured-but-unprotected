# State-law coding protocol

The legal panel is intentionally empty until each provision is verified against
primary legal sources. Do not code a state from an advocacy summary alone.

## Required procedure

1. Locate the enacted bill and codified statute on an official state website.
2. Record enactment and effective dates separately.
3. Determine whether the provision applies to individual-market QHPs.
4. Record exemptions, grandfathering, transition rules, and enforcement.
5. Code separate policy families rather than a single omnibus index.
6. Have a second reviewer verify treatment status and timing.

## Recommended initial scope

Begin with one policy family: either external-review reform or a tightly defined
prior-authorization procedural reform. A broad index combining unrelated laws
should not be the primary treatment without a defensible aggregation rule.

## Treatment timing

Treatment begins on the legally operative effective date. For annual outcomes,
document the exposure rule for midyear reforms (for example, first full calendar
year after effectiveness). Never use bill introduction or passage alone.
