# Paper outline

1. Introduction: hidden contract quality and the enforceability of coverage.
2. Institutional background: Marketplace plans, prior authorization, appeals, and ERISA boundaries.
3. Related literature: administrative frictions, insurance choice, denials, and state regulation.
4. Data: CMS Transparency PUFs, Marketplace plan/rate files, and legal coding.
5. Descriptive evidence: denial heterogeneity, appeals, premiums, participation, and legal adoption.
6. Empirical strategy: treatment definition, staggered timing, assumptions, and inference.
7. Results: access outcomes, premium responses, and participation responses.
8. Robustness and mechanisms.
9. Limits, external validity, and ERISA reach.
10. Conclusion.
